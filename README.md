# m154-hw5

For my research question, I wanted to see: 
Does the type of party show whether someone who uses AI will receive a 
professional sanction?
After running both decision tree and random forest models, I looked at their 
specificity, sensitivity, accuracy, and balanced accuracy. The decision tree had
somewhat of a better accuracy value, their sensitivity was 0 and specificity for 
the decision tree was 100%. As for their balanced accuracy, it was also pretty
similar. Both were not able to point out sanctioned cases but cases without
sanctions. My question wanted to look at professional sanctions yet the data has
more cases without sanctions then with.
From these two models, the type of party does not show whether someone who uses
AI will receive a professional sanction, or was not enough to show any true
results. If I were to do this again, I would use other predictors as well and
for ways to work around the disproportion of cases who did and did not result
in professional sanctions.
